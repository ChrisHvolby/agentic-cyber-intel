# Intelligence model

## Relevance
P1: assets or services explicitly designated by the adopter as high operational relevance.
P2: other in-scope enterprise assets.
P3: adjacent ecosystems or dependencies.

These are illustrative categories, not an inventory, severity rating or assertion of exposure. Critical events may override relevance according to privately defined criteria.

## Evidence
V1: full primary advisory inspected.
V2: official feed or partial primary evidence.
V3: credible attributed secondary evidence.
V4: unconfirmed early signal.

Grades describe evidence provenance, not guarantees of truth. Include material V2/V3 findings with uncertainty; V4 only when justified by private policy. Unknown fields remain unknown. Verify before remediation.

## Normalization
Use advisory/CVE/tracking identifiers where available. Capture publication, revision and observation timestamps, technical severity, prerequisites, affected/fixed versions, source links and recommended validation. Distinguish newly published findings from updates. Re-alert only for material changes when history permits. Report gaps rather than inferring absence.
